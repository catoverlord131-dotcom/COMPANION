============================================================
 COMPANION  v1.5
 A talking pixel pal for the TI-Nspire CX
============================================================

ABOUT
-----
Companion is a small AI pal that lives on your TI-Nspire CX.
You type into the box at the bottom of the screen, press Enter,
and Companion answers with a talking animation. It remembers
things about you and learns new words from what you type.

The whole program is one Lua script: Companion v1.5.lua

IMPORTANT NOTE: Code will ONLY work on a Nspire CX calculator

REQUIREMENTS
------------
  - TI-Nspire CX (or CX II) calculator
  - A computer with the TI-Nspire Computer Link app installed
  - The USB cable that came with the calculator
  - Companion saved as a .tns document (see the note below)


PUTTING COMPANION ON YOUR CALCULATOR (COMPUTER LINK)
----------------------------------------------------
 1. Turn on the calculator and plug it into the computer
    with the USB cable.

 2. Open the TI-Nspire Computer Link app on the computer.
    It should detect the calculator and show its files.

 3. Find Companion.tns on your computer.

 4. Drag Companion.tns into the calculator's file list
    in the Computer Link window (or use the app's send /
    transfer option). Wait for the transfer to finish.

 5. When the transfer is done, safely disconnect the
    calculator and unplug the cable.

 6. On the calculator, press the Home key, open My
    Documents, and open Companion.

Menu names can differ a little between versions of the app.
If something looks different, look for the option that sends
a file from the computer to the handheld.


USING COMPANION
---------------
Type in the box at the bottom and press Enter.

  Enter       Send your message
  Backspace   Delete the last character
  Esc         Clear what you typed; press again to skip
              the current reply
  Up arrow    Bring back your last message (when the box
              is empty)
  Tab         Insert spaces

Replies show one line at a time and page through by
themselves.

To keep Companion's memory, press ctrl+S now and then so the
document is saved.


WHAT COMPANION CAN DO
---------------------
  Chat
    About 195 topic groups with lots of replies each:
    moods, school, games, movies, music, food, sports,
    animals, science, history, space, tech, money, health,
    mythology and more.

  Dictionary
    About 730 words. Try:  define serendipity
    Or type a single word on its own.

  Quick facts
    Try:  how many bones in the human body
          what is the speed of light
          tallest mountain
          capital of peru

  Games, movies, shows, anime, music and books
    About 325 titles. Try:  who is mario
                            tell me about naruto

  Math
    12 * 7, powers, roots, factorials,
    factors of 360, 100 in binary, gcd of 12 and 18,
    is 97 prime

  Timers
    5 minute timer, or say: pomodoro

  Fun and games
    Jokes, riddles, facts, stories, coin flip, dice,
    rock paper scissors, would you rather, truth or dare,
    counting.

  Recommendations
    Ask for a game, movie or show suggestion.

  Memory
    Remembers your name, likes, goals and what you tell it.
    Ask:  what do you remember

  Learning
    Picks up words you use and mixes them into its answers.
    Teach it directly:  when i say X you say Y
    Ask:  what have you learned

  Personality
    A little clingy by default. Say "stop being clingy" or
    "be more clingy" to change it.

  Safety
    If you say you want to hurt yourself, Companion responds
    with care and points you to 988 (US).

Say "help" at any time for a short list of things to try.


TIPS
----
  - The more you tell Companion, the better it gets to
    know you.
  - Say things like "lol" or "thanks" when you like a reply.
    It notices.
  - If a reply feels off, tell it what it should have said
    next time.
